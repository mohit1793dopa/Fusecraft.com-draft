# Fusecrafts — Design Strategy

Aligned to brand guidelines (05.07.26), Emergent preview structure, and De Castelli–style editorial immersion.

## Positioning

Fusecrafts is a detail design and manufacturing studio for the in-betweens of architectural work. Founded by an architect who moved from building scale to object scale. Fusion is methodology: wood with brass, stone with rattan, industrial strength with hand finishing.

## Typography

- **Primary (headlines):** Switzer (GT Walsheim stand-in until licensed files arrive) — Bold / Medium
- **Secondary (body, UI, annotations):** Space Grotesk — Regular / Medium / Light
- Nav: Space Grotesk Medium, uppercase, tracked
- Buttons: Space Grotesk Medium

## Photography

Full-bleed workshop and furniture imagery. Material close-ups. No cluttered collages in the hero. Placeholders until client assets arrive.

## Motion (intentional, quiet luxury)

1. Hero: staggered fade-rise of eyebrow, headline, body, CTAs
2. In-view reveals on work tiles and material panels
3. Image subtle scale on hover / scroll
4. Nav underline / color transitions only — no bounce
